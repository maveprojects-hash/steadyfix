Get started in 5 minutes

A simple guide to your first day with STEADYFIX CMMS. Your 3-day free trial starts when you create your account. No card needed.

Start your free trialAsk us on WhatsApp

1Create your free account

  • Open steadyfixcmms.com and tap Log in, then create an account with your email and a password (8 or more characters).
  • Check your email for the confirmation message and tap the link. If you can't see it, look in your spam folder.
  • Log in. Then enter your company name, your full name and the name of your first site (for example Head office).

2Add your sites and your team

  • Tap Team & sites at the top right.
  • Add more sites if you have them, and invite your staff by email. They join with their own login.
  • Rosters don't need a login. Just type your staff names under Rosters, then Manage staff.

3Log your first job

  • Open Work tasks and tap + Add new.
  • Give it a title, a priority and a due date, and type who it is assigned to.
  • Add the parts and materials used, and the total adds up for you.
Tip: use Share on any list to send it to someone on WhatsApp, or Print for a paper copy.

4Set up your checklists

  • Open Checklists. Pick Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Yearly.
  • Add, rename or delete tasks so the list matches your property.
  • Tick items, add who checked it and who signed it off, then print the sheet with your letterhead.
  • Every checklist is saved. Find it again under Reports, then Saved checklists.

5Add your business details and logo

  • Open Settings. Upload your logo and fill in your name, addresses, VAT number and bank details.
  • Choose one of four layouts for quotes, invoices and orders, then tap Preview to see it.
  • From now on every printed sheet and document uses your details.

6Quotes, invoices and orders

  • Open Invoicing to add customers, create quotes and invoices, and turn a quote into an invoice in one tap.
  • Tap Email / Share PDF to send it to your customer.
  • Orders and damage reports use the same item lists, so you can add many items with prices.

7Install the app on your phone

  • Android (Chrome): open the Software tab and tap Install the app, or use the ⋮ menu and choose Install app.
  • iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

8Monitor your Wi-Fi and devices (PRO)

  • On the PRO plan, open the Software tab and download the computer app for Windows, Mac or Linux.
  • Install it on a computer that stays on, log in, and tap Yes to network scanning.
  • Your routers, access points and devices appear under Monitoring within a few minutes.

9After your trial

  • Your trial lasts 3 days. Then open the Subscribe tab and choose STANDARD ($75 per month) or PRO ($120 per month). You pay securely with PayPal.
  • Your plan switches on as soon as PayPal confirms the payment. You can cancel any time.
