Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy covers the STEADYFIX CMMS app and website, operated by Maverick's Projects ("we", "us").

What we collect

Your name, email address and company details when you sign up; the information you enter into the app (tasks, checklists, rosters, stock, damage reports, devices and readings, and so on); support requests you send; messages you send to our WhatsApp support number; and basic payment status from PayPal. We do not see or store your card or PayPal login details.

How we use it

To run the app for you, process your subscription, give support, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your information.

Who handles it for us

Supabase (database and sign-in), GitHub Pages (website hosting), PayPal (payments), Meta WhatsApp Business (WhatsApp support) and Anthropic (the AI that drafts WhatsApp support replies). These providers process data on our behalf.

Who can see your data

Only people in your own company who you have given access to. Other customers cannot see it. Our support staff may view a request you send us in order to answer it.

How long we keep it

While your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time. WhatsApp support messages are kept only as long as needed to give support.

Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and similar laws you may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information. Email support.easyfix@gmail.com.

Cookies and storage

The app stores your sign-in and a few settings (such as your print header) in your browser. We do not use advertising trackers.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and separated by company. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date above.
