**Full description:**
STEADYFIX CMMS is maintenance management for hotels, buildings and businesses. Run your daily jobs, keep your paperwork and prove the work was done, all from your phone or computer.

* Work tasks: assign to anyone by name, set priorities and due dates, see what is overdue
* Preventive maintenance schedules so nothing is missed
* Your own daily, weekly, monthly and yearly checklists, with a printable sheet showing your company name, who did the check and who signed it off
* Saved reports: every checklist is stored by date and can be printed again
* Rosters in a clear weekly or monthly grid with the day, date and staff
* Stock control, orders and shutdowns
* Damage reports for theft, breakages and insurance, with a printable signed sheet
* Device and Wi-Fi monitoring for meters, sensors, routers and phone systems, with alerts
* Budgets, expenses, fleet, suppliers and contractors
* Print, email or share any screen
* Many sites, each with its own staff and data

## Before you can publish in the stores
* **Right now the app is a website** that can be installed from Chrome (menu, "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"). That is ready to use today.
* **Google Play:** needs a Google Play developer account (about $25 once), a wrapper built from the website (PWABuilder.com can make one), a privacy policy link and the Data safety form. The wrapper also needs your own domain name, not the github.io address, so buy a domain and point it at the site first. New developer accounts must usually run a closed test with a minimum number of testers for about two weeks before going public. Check Google's current rules.
* **Apple App Store:** needs an Apple developer account (about $99 a year) and a Mac-built wrapper app. Apple often rejects apps that are only a website in a wrapper, and has its own rules about taking payment inside apps. Treat Apple as uncertain and start with the web app and Google Play.
