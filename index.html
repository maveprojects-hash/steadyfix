Questions

 

Do I need to install anything?

No. STEADYFIX runs in your browser. On a phone you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app. The PRO plan also includes the STEADYFIX computer app for Windows, Mac and Linux, with every download in one Software section, which runs together with the app on your phone.

How does the free trial work?

Create an account and use the app for 3 days. If you want to continue, choose a plan and pay by PayPal. Your information stays safe either way.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time in your PayPal account under Subscriptions.

Can the app be customized for our business?

Yes. Send us a customization request from the Support screen inside the app, or email us, and we will reply with options.

Is my data private?

Each company only ever sees its own data. Read our privacy policy.
